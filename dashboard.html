Pressure is a state—not a reputation

Classification uses the match equation before each delivery; outcomes and eventual winners are excluded.

Reproducible from supplied CSVs

Pressure rules

  • Chase: required rate ≥ 9.0; or ≥ 7.5 with 5+ wickets down; or 18+ needed from the final 12 balls.
  • First innings: 30 or fewer balls left with 4+ wickets down; or final 12 balls with 2+ down.
  • Required rate = runs needed ÷ legal balls remaining × 6. Phases and wickets-lost bands are included in baseline matching.

These thresholds represent a demanding scoring equation, scarce batting resources and late-innings leverage. First-innings pressure is a squeeze proxy because no target is available.

Performance and rating

Bat impact = batter runs − 8 × striker outs
Bowling impact = − bowler-charged runs + 8 × credited wickets

Compare pressure impact with the player's non-pressure rate in the same format, season, innings, phase and wicket band. Small baseline samples shrink toward the player's season-format rate, then cohort rate if needed.

Rating = lift₁₀₀ × n/(n + 80) × (0.75 + 0.25 × positive-match share)

Minimum exposure: 50 batter balls or 60 bowler legal balls, across 3+ matches. Wides/no-balls cost bowlers runs; byes/leg-byes do not.
